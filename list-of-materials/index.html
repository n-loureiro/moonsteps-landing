Overview

This page lists every material, product and fitting that BVC Arquitectura has already specified in the construction drawings package 260925_PublicacaoObra (phase “Execução (WIP)”, dated 25/09/2026). All 30 drawings and the drawing list were read. Brand names and references are copied exactly as they appear on the drawings.

Two drawings on the drawing list are marked “NÃO ENTREGUE” (not delivered): Cortes (sections) and Cozinha (kitchen). So there are no kitchen materials yet.

1. Bathrooms

Drawings 20.01–20.05. There are five bathrooms: 01.08, 01.09, 01.10 and 01.11 on the ground floor, and 02.04 in the attic.

2. Exterior windows and doors

Drawings 60.01–60.04.

  • Window and door frames: FINSTRAL PVC+PVC with thermal break, NOVA LINE 77, installed on a FIN-Fix sub-frame. Colour is satin grey ref. 46 (RAL 7044), with light-grey seals.
    • One fixed sash + one tilt-and-turn sash: 3 in the existing bedrooms, 2 in the new bedroom and office.
    • One tilt-and-turn sash: 3 in the kitchen and 4 in the bathrooms. One window in the upper bedroom is listed as “Sem estore” (no shutter).
    • VE.02.02 (3.15×1.00 m): sliding window with three sashes.
    • Window handles: standard Série 2, clear anodised aluminium (colour 79). Standard 4-point security lock and concealed hinges (opening up to 93°).
  • Glass: Plus-Valor double glazing, Uw 1.2 W/m²K, acoustic 32 dB. Some windows get “Body safe” tempered glass, others Multiprotect.
  • Living room sliding doors VE.02.01 (2.90×2.20 m), 2 units: FINSTRAL FIN-SLIDE Slim-line 144, two sliding sashes, Uw 1.4, lock with 2 hook latches.
  • Front door: FINSTRAL FIN DOOR Step Planar in aluminium, RAL 7044 matte (M744), with a fixed glazed side panel.
    • Glass: Multiprotect laminated safety glass, 38 dB.
    • Handles: Barra 70515 bar outside, Série 13 inside.
    • Locking: class 2 security cylinder, 5-point locking.
    • Also: 2 cm threshold and a door viewer (peephole).
  • Shutters: extruded aluminium security shutters, RAL 7044 finish.
  • VELUX roof windows (white polyurethane, electric, remote control):
    • GPU-SK06 panoramic, attic bedroom, with electric blackout blind and exterior awning
    • GGU-UK04, attic bathroom, with exterior awning
    • GGU-SK06, stairs

Drawings 30.01–30.02.

6. Equipment and electrical

Drawing 70.01 and the floor plans.

  • Wood-burning stove (recuperador de calor): C&A CHAMA, Cristal 69, with side ventilation.
  • Switches and sockets: EFAPEL SIZA series.
  • Roof equipment: flat solar panels, 1 heat pump and 3 air-conditioning units. No models are given.
  • Lighting: only a legend of light types (recessed spot, IP65 recessed spot, LED in a ceiling recess, wall light, outdoor surface spot, suspended light bar, pendant points). No product models.
  • Kitchen symbols on the ground-floor plan: FR, FG/EX, MLL, LL, FN, MO, MLR, MSR, with no specification. A likely reading is fridge, hob/extractor, dishwasher, sink, oven, microwave, washing machine, dryer (an interpretation, not stated on the drawing).

7. Walls, ceilings, skirting

Drawings 01.05, 01.06 and the floor plans.

  • Walls:
    • Reinforced concrete structure.
    • 250 mm thermal/acoustic block for new walls; 150 mm structural concrete block.
    • Outside: 50 mm external insulation (capotto) on new walls; 20 mm smooth thermal render on existing walls.
    • Inside face of exterior walls: rock wool + plasterboard (50 mm on existing walls, 30 mm on new walls).
    • Partitions: plasterboard with rock wool; some on a light-steel (LSF) frame with at least 50 mm rock wool.
  • Ceilings: plasterboard false ceilings at +2.70 m; moisture-resistant at +2.60 m in the bathrooms. In the attic, the ceiling follows the roof slope.
  • Skirting: projecting wooden skirting, to be painted.

8. Facade and roof

Drawings 80.01–80.02.

  • Roof: Telha Lusa tiles (matching the existing), breathable roof membrane, OSB board, light-steel structure, zinc flashings and zinc gutters.
  • Facade:
    • New facade: capotto painted white.
    • Existing facade: thermal render painted yellow (existing colour).
  • Stone: stone window surrounds, stone plinth, stone sill and stone threshold.
  • Flat roof: waterproofing membranes with washed gravel on top.
  • Floor build-up: screed with underfloor heating, waterproofing and a geodrain behind the foundations.
