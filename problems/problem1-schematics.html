Scope and changes

This is a first-attempt set of schematics for the emissometer described in the problem formulation, sized for a rigid sample of 50 × 50 × 5 mm. It fixes enough dimensions to estimate loads, order long-lead items and start detailed CAD. It is not a drawing package: nothing here has tolerance stack-ups, fastener calls or weld details beyond what Table 4 lists.

Three things changed from the formulation while sizing it:

  • Cold head on top. A pulse tube must run cold end down, so the cold head hangs from a fixed top plate and the vacuum can, shield and shroud drop away on guide rods. The formulation had it standing in the chamber base; that page is corrected.
  • Feedthroughs. A station needs 26 wires, not about 16, so the cryostat has four 41-pin connectors (one per station) plus a 32-pin connector for housekeeping.
  • Grease instead of indium. Indium needs MPa-level clamping, which a face-clamped sample cannot get without large masks. A ~25 µm Apiezon film (N up to 300 K, H up to 370 K) under four corner clips gives 0.17 K across the joint at the worst case.

Cryostat stack-up

The radial build-up from the centre is: tower half-width 55, standoffs to 75, guard to 101 (the sample face), 97 mm of free view to the shroud absorber, shroud wall at 200–203, 27 mm to the shield, MLI, and the can at 255. Vertically, the top plate is 0–25 mm, the 1st-stage plate sits at 120, the 2nd-stage plate at 230, the sample centres at 440 (the middle of the shroud) and the can bottom at 718.

Sample station

The sample's back sits on the carrier through the grease film; its edges face the guard frame across 0.3 mm; its face is the only surface that sees the shroud. The carrier hangs on eight Kevlar threads, about 3.7×10⁻⁶ W/K in total, so the threads matter only if the guard drifts. Flushness is set by shimming the frame, not by moving the carrier.

Wiring

The two chromel legs of each differential thermocouple run to adjacent pins of the same connector so the chromel–pin junctions sit at the same temperature and cancel. Heater current leads are 32 AWG and measured: at 224 mA, half their Joule heating (about 5 mW) lands on the carrier and is subtracted.

Sample loading and first cooldown

Loading a 50 × 50 × 5 mm sample, with the cryostat warm and vented with dry N₂:

  1. Lower the can, shield and shroud on the guide rods. Wear gloves from here on.
  2. Remove the guard frame (four M2 screws) and the four clips.
  3. Clean the carrier face with isopropanol. Spread about 0.06 g of grease (25 µm over 2500 mm²): Apiezon N if the run stays at or below 300 K, Apiezon H if it goes to 370 K.
  4. Seat the sample and slide it a few millimetres back and forth to wet the film. Align its edges with the carrier within 0.1 mm.
  5. Place the front thermocouple junction on one corner with a 25 µm Kapton pad over it, then fit the clips at 0.1 N·m.
  6. Refit the frame. Check flushness at the four mid-sides with the dial-gauge jig (±0.05 mm) and the gap with a 0.25 / 0.35 mm feeler pair. Adjust shims until both pass.
  7. Check every sensor resistance and insulation to ground above 100 MΩ.
  8. Raise the shroud, shield and can; pump below 1e-5 mbar; start cooldown.

The first cooldown runs with gold-plated dummy samples instead of real ones: one 50 × 50 and one 25 × 25 on gold, in different stations. Plotting P against area at each temperature gives the slope (gold ε) and the intercept (P_par). That intercept is the parasitic term every later measurement subtracts.

Open items

  • Cold-head choice and its load map at 2nd-stage temperatures of 60–100 K with about 14 W vendor.
  • Cooldown time for about 15 kg on the 2nd stage. A gas-gap heat switch between the plates would precool it through the 1st stage decide.
  • Buckling of the Ø510 × 4 mm can under external pressure calculate.
  • Far-IR absorptance of the coating on a test coupon before the shroud is coated test.
  • Kevlar creep over repeated 30–370 K cycles: re-tension check after each warm-up procedure.
  • Thick insulating samples need the front thermocouple; without it they are outside the U < 0.04 claim customer.
